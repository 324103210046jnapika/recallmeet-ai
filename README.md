# RecallMeet AI



## AI Meeting Intelligence Agent with Persistent Memory



**Hackathon Theme:** AI Agents That Learn Using Hindsight



RecallMeet AI is an AI meeting-intelligence agent designed to turn previous client interactions into usable experience for future meetings.



Instead of treating every meeting as an isolated conversation, RecallMeet maintains client-specific interaction history, retrieves relevant memories, identifies commitments and unresolved concerns, and generates a focused preparation brief for the next interaction.



\---



## The Problem



Important client context is often scattered across previous meetings:



\- Decisions get forgotten

\- Technical preferences are repeated

\- Commitments become difficult to track

\- Unresolved concerns can remain hidden

\- Teams spend time reconstructing relationship history before every meeting



A conventional meeting summarizer primarily answers:



> "What happened in this meeting?"



RecallMeet asks a different question:



> "What have we learned from this client over time, and what should we remember before the next meeting?"
